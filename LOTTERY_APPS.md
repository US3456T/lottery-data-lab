# Lottery analysis app shortlist (2026-11)

This is a research shortlist, not a claim that these are the world's 20 best or most intelligent products. Public-facing lottery products are mostly statistical dashboards; only a small number market themselves as AI/ML. Marketing claims are not independent evidence that a system learns effectively or predicts random draws. Check availability, pricing, privacy, and your lottery game's coverage on the official site before using any service.

**Important:** Lottery outcomes are designed to be random. Historical frequency, AI labels, scores, and backtests do not establish an edge or improve the odds of a future draw. Do not treat any listed product as betting advice.

## Selection notes

- This list is a set of leads for evaluation, **not an independently audited ranking**. Availability and platform details can change; confirm them directly before installing, paying, or sharing data.
- The first seven products explicitly market themselves as AI/AI-assisted in the public pages surfaced during research. That does not verify their model architecture, training process, or predictive performance.
- Items 8–9 and the statistical products are included as adjacent candidates, not represented as proven AI apps. A credible list of 20 independently verified, genuinely learning lottery AI apps could not be substantiated from the available public evidence.
- The project in this repository is a local historical-data analysis dashboard; this catalog does not install or integrate these third-party services.
